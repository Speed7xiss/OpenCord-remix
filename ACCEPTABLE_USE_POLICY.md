# OpenCord Acceptable Use Policy

Version: 2026-08-23

OpenCord is intended for lawful communication and community hosting. You may not use OpenCord, a OpenCord instance, or project-controlled services to facilitate, coordinate, encourage, or conceal unlawful or seriously abusive activity.

## Prohibited uses

The following uses are prohibited:

- Unauthorized access to accounts, devices, networks, services, or data
- Credential theft, phishing, session theft, token theft, or trafficking in stolen credentials
- Malware distribution, ransomware, destructive code, botnets, cryptojacking, or command-and-control activity
- Denial-of-service attacks, service disruption, network abuse, or coordinated attack traffic
- Doxxing, swatting, stalking, extortion, coercion, credible threats, or targeted harassment
- Fraud, scams, impersonation for unlawful gain, money laundering, or trafficking in stolen property
- Distribution or solicitation of child sexual abuse material or sexual exploitation of minors
- Human trafficking, illegal weapons trafficking, or unlawful controlled-substance trafficking
- Sharing unlawfully obtained private data or facilitating identity theft
- Copyright or trademark infringement at a scale or in a manner prohibited by applicable law
- Attempts to use the project maintainers, project branding, or project-controlled channels to legitimize unlawful activity
- Deliberately bypassing moderation, account restrictions, or security controls for an unlawful purpose

Security research performed with authorization, defensive administration, interoperability work, and lawful testing on systems you own or have permission to test are not prohibited by this policy.

## Instance operators

Independent instance operators are responsible for establishing and enforcing rules appropriate to their community and jurisdiction. Operators should provide a reporting mechanism, respond to credible abuse reports, preserve evidence when legally appropriate, and comply with valid legal obligations applicable to them.

The OpenCord maintainers do not automatically receive third-party instance reports, messages, databases, or logs and cannot moderate an instance they do not operate.

## Enforcement on project-controlled services

For services and communities directly operated by the OpenCord maintainers, violations may result in content removal, loss of access, refusal of support, or reporting to appropriate authorities when legally required or reasonably necessary to address an imminent threat.

## Reporting

Security vulnerabilities in OpenCord should be reported according to `SECURITY.md`. Abuse occurring on an independently hosted instance should normally be reported to that instance operator or the appropriate service/network provider.
