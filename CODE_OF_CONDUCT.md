# Code of Conduct

Project-controlled communities and contribution spaces should remain professional and safe.

Do not harass, threaten, stalk, dox, discriminate against, or deliberately expose private information about another person. Do not use project spaces to coordinate unlawful activity, distribute malware, steal credentials, commit fraud, or target third parties.

Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these rules. Good-faith technical disagreement, criticism, security review, and discussion of defensive security are welcome when conducted constructively.
